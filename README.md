# ES‑MEGA‑ALU Energy System  
### Modular, Emission‑Free, High‑Power Aluminum‑Water Energy Architecture

The ES‑MEGA‑ALU Energy System is a sovereign, next‑generation energy ecosystem designed for 
industrial‑scale power generation, full circularity, and radical transparency. 
By harvesting energy from the controlled reaction of aluminum and water, 
it provides electricity, hydrogen, and thermal energy without harmful emissions.
